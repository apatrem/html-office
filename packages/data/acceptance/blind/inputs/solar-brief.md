# Brief: ten-year model of the Ridgeway community solar roof

*Fictional brief for a test: the co-operative, the roof and every figure are invented.*

The Ridgeway Energy Co-operative is deciding whether to build a solar roof on the village hall. The board wants a small workbook it can open in a browser, change the assumptions in and share with members. Euros, British English, one column per year from 2027 to 2036.

## Assumptions

| Assumption | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Panels | 360 |
| Power per panel | 0.43 kWp |
| Yield in 2027 | 1,020 kWh per kWp |
| Output loss (degradation) | 0.5% a year: each year produces 99.5% of the year before |
| Price of electricity sold, EUR per kWh | Low 0.085, Base 0.10, High 0.12 |
| Maintenance (O&M) | EUR 3,800 in 2027, rising 2% a year |
| Insurance | EUR 1,100 a year |
| Building cost | EUR 85,000, paid on 1 January 2027 |

The price depends on the scenario: the board wants a dropdown to choose Low, Base or High, with Base selected.

## What the workbook must show

- For each year: electricity produced (kWh), revenue, maintenance, insurance, cash flow (revenue minus maintenance and insurance) and cumulative cash, starting from minus the building cost.
- A summary with the total revenue over ten years, the cumulative cash at the end of 2036 and the first year in which cumulative cash is positive (or "not within ten years").
- A chart of the yearly cash flow and the cumulative cash.
- Negative cumulative cash highlighted.

Do not add taxes, financing or inflation beyond what is listed: the board will add them later.
