# Risks and lessons {#sec-risks .new-page}

The board reviews the risk register every quarter. Four risks need the members' attention because they shape the 2027 plan; the full register is available to members on request. {.lead}

**Wholesale capacity.** The Docks bakehouse cannot grow further without a third night shift, which the bakers voted against in October. Until Riverside opens, any new wholesale contract must replace an existing one or be baked at Old Town in the afternoon, which affects quality. *Response:* Riverside opening in September 2027; no new wholesale contract larger than EUR 100,000 a year before then.

**Energy and the move to electric ovens.** Gas prices may rise again when the fixed tariff ends in September 2028. Electric deck ovens cost more to buy but less to run, and the co-operative's own solar panels at the Docks can cover part of the daytime load. *Response:* the Riverside line is electric from the start; Old Town follows in 2028, financed from reserves.

**The Westgate shop.** Westgate has lost money for two years. Its lease runs until 2029. Shorter hours since August have cut the loss by about half, but the shop still does not cover its costs. *Response:* the members will decide by the end of June whether to keep, move or close the shop, on the basis of the first half's figures.

**Dependence on two large customers.** The school meals service and the café chain now make up 11% of revenue. Losing either would hurt, although the school contract runs until 2029. *Response:* no single wholesale customer above 8% of revenue by the end of 2027; three smaller contracts in negotiation.

**Payments and systems.** All four shops, the online shop and the subscription system depend on card payments and a few online services. A two-hour card outage in March cost about EUR 6,000 of sales. *Response:* a second payment provider for the shops, offline card payments enabled, and a written plan for an outage of the online shop.

> [!NOTE]
> What we learned in 2026. Planning production a week ahead works better than taking orders the evening before; small price rises twice a year are accepted better than one large one; and the cargo bikes are cheaper and more reliable than we expected, but need a covered depot for winter.

The board also notes one lesson about the way it reports. In 2025 the members asked for figures by shop and for a simpler budget table. This review includes both, and Appendix B gives the main facts for each site ([[xref: app-sites]]).
