# Appendix C. Questions from the members' evenings {#app-questions .unnumbered .new-page}

Two open evenings, on 14 and 28 October, discussed a draft of this plan. About sixty members came to each. The main questions and the board's answers are summarised here; the full notes are available to members on request.

**Why open a new bakehouse rather than add a third night shift at the Docks?** The bakers voted against a third shift in October, and the board agrees: night work is hard to staff and to combine with family life. A second wholesale bakehouse also protects the co-operative if one site has to stop, as the Docks did for four days in June after the oven failure.

**Is Riverside too large for our needs?** The building is larger than the bakehouse needs today. The lease allows the co-operative to sublet the upper floor, and a local theatre company has asked to rent it as a rehearsal space. The plan does not count on that income.

**Why electric ovens, when gas is cheaper this year?** The fixed gas tariff ends in 2028 and nobody can say what gas will cost then. Electric ovens cost about EUR 60,000 more for the Riverside line but less to run, and the solar panels at the Docks will be joined by panels on the Riverside roof. The board will show the comparison again before deciding on Old Town in 2028.

**Will prices rise again?** The budget assumes rises of about 2% in March and 2% in September in the shops, below the expected rise in costs. Wholesale prices follow the flour index, as the contracts say.

**What happens to Westgate's staff if the shop closes?** No worker-member will lose their job. If the members decide to close or move the shop, its six colleagues will be offered places at Riverside, Mill Lane or Hillcrest, as the co-operative's rules require.

**Can members see the figures by shop during the year?** Yes. From 2027 the quarterly report will add revenue, costs and profit for each shop and bakehouse to the tables by channel and by cost line used in this review ([[xref: tab-revenue]], [[xref: tab-costs]]).

**Could the subscription boxes include products from other local producers?** A pilot with a cheese maker and a fruit farm will start in the spring for subscribers who ask for it. It will be reported separately so that its margin is clear.
