# Appendix B. Sites at a glance {#app-sites .unnumbered .new-page}

**Mill Lane** (shop and small bakehouse, opened 2011): the first and busiest shop, with the breakfast counter since 2025; 22 people; revenue EUR 2.1 million.

**Station Road** (shop, opened 2015): commuters and the station's offices, open from 6:30; 14 people; revenue EUR 1.4 million.

**Hillcrest** (shop, opened 2018): a residential district with many families; strongest at weekends; 12 people; revenue EUR 1.3 million.

**Westgate** (shop, opened 2021): shorter hours since August 2026; 6 people; revenue EUR 0.8 million and a small loss.

**Old Town** (bakehouse, since 2013): supplies the shops and the subscription boxes; start of the cargo-bike loop; 31 people.

**Docks** (wholesale bakehouse, since 2019): night baking for cafés, schools and restaurants, solar panels on the roof; 34 people.

**Riverside** (bakehouse, shop and bike depot, opening September 2027): see [[xref: sec-priorities]].
