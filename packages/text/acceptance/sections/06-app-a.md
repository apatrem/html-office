# Appendix A. Definitions and sources {#app-definitions .unnumbered .new-page}

**Revenue** is sales net of VAT and of discounts, including delivery charges. Subscription revenue is recognised in the week the box is delivered.

**Operating costs** exclude interest, tax and the patronage refund. One-off costs are included in the year they are spent and named in the text when they are material.

**Operating profit** is revenue less operating costs. The **margin** is operating profit divided by revenue.

**Worker-members** are people employed by the co-operative who have been admitted as members after their trial period. **Turnover** is the share of people who left during the year.[^def-turnover]

**Sources.** Sales figures come from the shop tills, the wholesale invoicing system and the online shop; costs from the accounting ledger as at 31 October, with November and December forecast. Customer satisfaction comes from the quarterly survey (about 900 answers a quarter). All figures, names and places in this document are fictional and were invented for the html-text acceptance document; any resemblance to a real business is a coincidence.

[^def-turnover]: People who left during the year divided by the average number of members and employees over the year.
