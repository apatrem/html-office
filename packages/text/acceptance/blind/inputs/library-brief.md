# Brief: annual report 2026 of the Northbrook Library Network

*Fictional brief for a test: the library network, its branches and every figure are invented.*

The board of the Northbrook Library Network wants its annual report for 2026 as one document that the trustees can read in a browser, comment on and print. About six to eight pages, in British English, for trustees and the public.

## What the report must contain

1. **Summary**: the year in one page, with the key message in a highlighted box: visits rose 6% to 811 thousand, and e-book loans grew fastest.
2. **Visits**: visits by branch for 2025 and 2026, as a column chart, and a short commentary per branch.
3. **Loans**: loans by category, as a table whose header has "Loans (thousands)" spanning the 2025 and 2026 columns, and a total row.
4. **Budget for 2027**: the budget by line, as a table with a total row, and what changes.
5. **Next steps**: three or four priorities for 2027.

The summary must point to the chart and to the loans table with cross-references. Give the sources and caveats below as footnotes. Mark the document as fictional on its cover. Add a contents list and numbered headings.

## Notes from the network manager

- Harbour closed for three weeks in March 2026 for roof repairs, which explains its fall in visits. Visits are counted by door sensors at each entrance.
- E-book loans include the regional consortium platform, which the network joined in January 2026.
- Northgate opened on Sundays from September 2026, a pilot to be reviewed in June 2027.
- The digital services line includes the replacement of the self-service machines at Central.
- Priorities the board has agreed: extend Sunday opening if the Northgate pilot works, a homework club at Eastfield, the Harbour roof's second phase, and a review of the adult fiction collection.
