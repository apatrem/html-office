# Proposal: cargo-bike deliveries for the east district

*A fictional draft for a test: the co-operative, the district and every figure are invented.*

Prepared by the logistics group for the board, November 2026.

## Why the east district

The east district holds a quarter of our subscription customers but is served by a single van that leaves the Docks at six and returns at eleven. Deliveries there are late more often than anywhere else: one in eight arrived after the promised window in October.[^late] Parking near the new housing on Canal Street is difficult, and the city plans a low-traffic zone there from 2028.

Customers notice. In the autumn survey, *punctual delivery* was the first request of east-district subscribers, ahead of more choice and lower prices.

## What we propose

We propose to replace the east-district van round with two cargo bikes, loaded at a small depot behind the Mill Lane shop:

- the van brings the morning's bread to Mill Lane at half past five, as it already does for the shop;
- two riders leave Mill Lane at six and serve the district in two loops of about 14 kilometres each;
- the van is freed for wholesale deliveries, where we turn away orders on Saturdays.

> The west-district loop shows that this works: since March, 97% of its deliveries have been on time.

## Costs and savings

| Item | Per year (EUR) |
| --- | --- |
| Two cargo bikes, leased | 9,600 |
| Riders (two part-time posts) | 41,000 |
| Depot rent and fit-out | 4,800 |
| Van costs saved | −28,500 |

The net cost is about EUR 27,000 a year, before the extra wholesale sales the van could make on Saturdays.[^sales] The logistics group expects the bikes to pay for themselves within two years if half of those sales materialise.

## Risks

Cargo bikes are slower in heavy rain and snow; on the west loop the van replaced the bikes on four days last winter. Riders need training and a back-up rota. The depot behind Mill Lane must be secured against theft.[^insurance]

## Decision requested

The board is asked to approve a six-month trial from March 2027, with a report to the members' council in October.

[^late]: Delivery times are logged by the drivers' handsets; a delivery is late when it arrives more than fifteen minutes after the end of the window.
[^sales]: Wholesale orders refused on Saturdays averaged EUR 1,100 a week in 2026.
[^insurance]: The co-operative's insurer has asked for a locked cage and a camera before it covers the bikes at the depot.
