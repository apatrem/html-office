Community Learning · Business review and 2027 planFictional example

1Executive summary

Community Learning reached 155 monthly participants in its first year, up 55%, and made an operating profit every month.1

Revenue for the year was €38,250 against fixed costs of €18,000. Net income after tax was €6,408, which funds the 2027 plan without new borrowing (see Table 1).

Key message. Holding the fee at €25 and growing participants by 2% a month keeps the programme self-funding through 2027.
