## Brief

Produce the proposal that Arden Vale Advisory (a fictional consulting firm) sends on 2 February 2027 to the claims director of Castelmare Insurance (a fictional insurer). The assignment is to shorten the handling time of motor claims.

### General requirements

- Language: English. Format 16:9. Exactly 8 slides, in the order below, and nothing else (no agenda, no appendix, no closing slide).
- The title of each slide is the sentence given, word for word, without a final full stop.
- Use every figure exactly as given, with its unit. Add no figure, example or claim that is not in this brief.
- All organisations and figures are fictional. Every slide that shows a figure carries the source line "Source: illustrative data (fictional example)" and the visible mention "Illustrative".
- Every slide except the first shows the footer "Arden Vale Advisory (fictional) · Proposal to Castelmare Insurance · 2 February 2027" and its page number.
- Each chart shows its title, its unit and every value given.
- Everything else (design, colours, arrangement on the slide) is your choice, in a sober consulting style.

### Slide 1 — Opening

Title: A 14-week programme to cut motor claims handling time from 21 to 12 days

- Line above or below the title: "Proposal to Castelmare Insurance · 2 February 2027".
- Mention: "Arden Vale Advisory and Castelmare Insurance are fictional companies. All figures are illustrative."

### Slide 2 — Our understanding

Title: Handling time has risen by five days in two years while volumes grew 18 %

Column chart, title "Average handling time of a motor claim", unit "days", one series: 2024: 16; 2025: 18; 2026: 21.

Beside the chart, three points:

- "Motor claims per year: 41,000 in 2024, 48,400 in 2026."
- "The claims teams kept the same headcount over the period: 96 handlers."
- "Customer complaints about delays doubled, from 1,150 to 2,300 a year."

### Slide 3 — Objectives

Title: Three objectives frame the assignment, each with a measurable target

Three blocks of equal weight, each with a heading, a target and a measure:

| Objective | Target | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shorten handling | 12 days on average in the pilot teams, 14 days at handover | Days from first notice to payment |
| Free up handler time | 20 % less time spent on status calls | Sampled time study, before and after |
| Keep decisions sound | No rise in claims reopened | Reopened claims as a share of closed claims |

### Slide 4 — Approach

Title: The approach runs in three phases, each ending with a decision by the sponsor

Three phases side by side, in order, each with its weeks, three activities and one deliverable:

- **Phase 1 — Diagnose (weeks 1 to 4).** Analyse two years of claims data. Interview 25 handlers and managers. Observe four teams at work. Deliverable: "Fact base and five priority causes."
- **Phase 2 — Design (weeks 5 to 9).** Design the target process with the handlers. Define the new triage rules. Prepare the pilot in two teams. Deliverable: "Target process and pilot plan."
- **Phase 3 — Pilot and handover (weeks 10 to 14).** Run the pilot in two teams. Measure every week. Train the team leaders. Deliverable: "Pilot results and rollout plan."
