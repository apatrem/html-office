## Brief

Produce the five-slide summary of a branch network review that the head of retail banking of Quillmere Bank (a fictional bank) sends to the executive committee before its meeting of 18 February 2027. The committee reads it beforehand: the slides carry the full reasoning in text, and are not spoken over.

### General requirements

- Language: English. Format 16:9. Exactly 5 slides, in the order below, and nothing else (no agenda, no appendix, no closing slide).
- The title of each slide is the sentence given, word for word, without a final full stop.
- Use every figure exactly as given, with its unit. Add no figure, example or claim that is not in this brief.
- All organisations and figures are fictional. Every slide that shows a figure carries the source line "Source: illustrative data (fictional example)" and the visible mention "Illustrative".
- Every slide except the first shows the footer "Quillmere Bank (fictional) · Branch network review · Executive committee of 18 February 2027" and its page number.
- Each chart shows its title, its unit and every value given.
- Everything else (design, colours, arrangement on the slide) is your choice, in a sober consulting style.

Every sentence quoted below appears in full on its slide: this is a document to read, and the text is not to be shortened.

### Slide 1 — Summary

Title: Closing 38 of 212 branches saves €21.0 million a year and keeps 96 % of customers within 15 minutes of a branch

- Mention, small: "Quillmere Bank is a fictional company. All figures are illustrative."
- Paragraph headed "Situation": "Customers visit branches far less than four years ago, and most everyday operations are now done online or by phone. The network of 212 branches was sized for the visits of 2022. Its cost per visit has risen by 55 % since then."
- Three findings, as a list:
  - "Visits fell by 41 % between 2022 and 2026, while digital contacts tripled."
  - "38 branches combine low activity, a nearby alternative and a lease that ends soon."
  - "Closing them saves €21.0 million a year, net of the revenue lost with customers who leave."
- A separate, clearly visible box headed "Recommendation": "Close the 38 branches between June and December 2027, redeploy 60 % of their staff, and keep a service point wherever the branch was the last one in its town."

### Slide 2 — What changed

Title: Branch visits have fallen by 41 % in four years while digital contacts have tripled

Table:

| Year | Branch visits (million) | Digital contacts (million) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2022 | 14.6 | 31 |
| 2024 | 11.2 | 58 |
| 2026 | 8.6 | 93 |

Two text blocks beside or below the table.

Block headed "What changed":

- "Nine operations in ten are now done without a branch, against six in ten in 2022."
- "Cash withdrawals at the counter fell by 63 %."
- "Customers over 70 still make 2.4 times as many visits as the average customer."
- "Mortgage and business appointments held up: they are 28 % of visits, against 15 % in 2022."

Block headed "What it means":

- "Branches are used less for transactions and more for advice."
- "A smaller network must keep advisers within reach, not counters."
- "Older customers need an alternative for cash before any closure."

### Slide 3 — How the branches were chosen

Title: The 38 branches were selected on three criteria, applied in the same way across all regions

Three criteria, each with its definition and its threshold:

1. **Low activity.** "Fewer than 45 visits a day on average in 2026, against 164 for the network."
2. **A nearby alternative.** "Another Quillmere branch within 15 minutes by car for at least 90 % of the branch's customers."
3. **A lease that ends soon.** "Lease ending or breakable before the end of 2028, so that leaving costs less than staying."

Paragraph headed "Result": "61 branches meet the three criteria. 23 of them are the last branch in a town of more than 10,000 inhabitants and stay open as they are. The 38 others are proposed for closure. No region loses more than a quarter of its branches."

### Slide 4 — What it saves

Title: The saving is net of the revenue at risk, and the one-off costs are recovered in about five months

Table:

| Item | € million a year |
| --- | --- |
| Property and running costs saved | 14.5 |
| Staff costs saved, after redeploying 60 % of the staff | 11.0 |
| Revenue at risk: 3 % of the affected customers leave | −4.5 |
| Net saving | 21.0 |

A separate box headed "One-off costs": "Leaving the leases costs €5.5 million, and redundancy and retraining cost €3.5 million: €9.0 million in total, recovered in about five months of savings."

Paragraph headed "Sensitivity": "If 6 % of the affected customers left instead of 3 %, the net saving would be €16.5 million a year. The closures would still pay back within seven months."

### Slide 5 — Decisions

Title: Three decisions are needed from the executive committee on 18 February

Three decisions, numbered, each with its owner and its date:

1. "Approve the closure of the 38 branches between June and December 2027." Owner: head of retail banking. Date: 18 February.
2. "Fund the one-off costs of €9.0 million from the 2027 transformation budget." Owner: chief financial officer. Date: 18 February.
3. "Open the consultation with staff representatives." Owner: head of human resources. Date: 1 March.

Paragraph headed "Main risk": "Local reaction in the towns concerned. Each closure is announced at least four months ahead, with the alternative service described branch by branch."

## Three later edits

Slide numbers are those of the deck before these changes.

1. **Updated figures, wherever they appear.** The property estimate has been revised: "Property and running costs saved" is €13.0 million a year instead of €14.5 million, so the net saving is €19.5 million instead of €21.0 million. On slide 4, update the two rows of the table; in the box "One-off costs", the end of the sentence becomes "recovered in about five and a half months of savings."; the paragraph "Sensitivity" becomes: "If 6 % of the affected customers left instead of 3 %, the net saving would be €15.0 million a year. The closures would still pay back within eight months." The title of slide 4 becomes: "The saving is net of the revenue at risk, and the one-off costs are recovered in about five and a half months". On slide 1, the title becomes "Closing 38 of 212 branches saves €19.5 million a year and keeps 96 % of customers within 15 minutes of a branch" and the third finding "Closing them saves €19.5 million a year, net of the revenue lost with customers who leave."
2. **A new slide after slide 3.** Title: "Customers of the closing branches keep access to the bank through three alternatives". Three text blocks of equal weight, each with a heading and a sentence: "Nearest branch" — "On average 7 minutes further by car; appointments with the same adviser for the first year."; "Mobile adviser" — "Two days a week in 12 towns, in a room rented from the town hall."; "Cash at the post office" — "Withdrawals and deposits at the post office counter in 29 of the 38 locations." The slide carries the source line, the mention "Illustrative", the footer and its page number like the others; the slides after it are renumbered.
3. **A changed date.** The executive committee meets on 25 February instead of 18 February. The title of slide 5 becomes: "Three decisions are needed from the executive committee on 25 February"; the dates of decisions 1 and 2 become 25 February; the footer of every slide becomes "Quillmere Bank (fictional) · Branch network review · Executive committee of 25 February 2027".
