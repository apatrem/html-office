Summary

Three small changes reduce weekly coordination time from 30 to 24 hours

A clear rota, shared tools and a simple task board make routine work easier.

  • Group tasks by day so volunteers know what to expect.
  • Keep frequently used tools in a labelled shared area.
  • Review the rota together after the first month.
